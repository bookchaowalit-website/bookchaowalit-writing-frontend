# Design system — The Writing Desk

## Overview

A proofing desk for a small reading list. The interface treats each note as a piece of copy that made it through an edit, not as a generic content card.

## Colors

- Proof paper: #F2EEE4
- Ink: #202A2A
- Desk panel: #DFE3DC
- Editorial red: #C55A43
- Archive blue: #355D70
- Hairline: #C7C2B6

## Typography

- Display and entries: Palatino Linotype / Book Antiqua / Georgia.
- Labels, issue numbers, and dates: Courier New.
- Avenir Next carries search and explanatory copy.
- Red is correction/editorial emphasis; blue is metadata.

## Layout

- Open paper hero with a desk-note slip on the right.
- Proofing table contains search, status filters, a ruled index, and static-boundary note.
- Entry rows carry issue number, status, title, summary, and date.
- Mobile stacks metadata under the reading copy.

## Elevation & Depth

No shadows. The tinted proofing panel and strict rules supply the work surface.

## Shapes

Straight edges, underlines, and rectangular controls. No pill buttons or cards.

## Components

- Writing header with static-index status.
- Desk-note slip.
- Proofing controls and status filters.
- Entry row with reading-copy metadata.
- Empty-proof recovery state.

## Do’s and Don’ts

Do foreground the sentence and the reading path. Do keep the demo boundary visible.
Don’t imply an editor, private drafts, publishing pipeline, or multi-user CMS.
